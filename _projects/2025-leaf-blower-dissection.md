As part of the MAE 3230 Fluid Mechanics course, our final project involved dissecting an everyday item that utilizes fluid mechanical devices and producing an explanatory video. My group chose to reverse-engineer a leaf blower, which consists of centrifugal pumps, internal piping, fluid containers, and a two-stroke engine. I loved having the opportunity to get my hands dirty (literally) to see how the concepts I learned in lecture could be utilized to make everyday tasks easier.

### Centrifugal Pump Analysis
Beyond video production, my primary technical contribution was conducting the analysis of the leaf blower's centrifugal pump. We disassembled the unit to examine the internal impeller and volute casing to understand how it accelerates the air.
